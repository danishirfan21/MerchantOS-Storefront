## Brand & Style
The design system establishes a high-tier, customer-facing consumer electronics storefront engineered for discernment, clarity, and trust in the Saudi Arabian premium retail market. The brand personality balances architectural restraint with retail momentum: authoritative, technologically sophisticated, pristine, and effortless.

The design movement combines **Contemporary Minimalism** with **Tactile Precision**. The UI prioritizes visual breathing room, deliberate negative space, and crisp product isolation to showcase flagship devices, smart appliances, and audio gear without visual noise. Surfaces maintain a luminous off-white cast that feels tactile and editorial rather than sterile. Every interactive transition, hover elevation, and visual anchor instills transactional confidence, elevating consumer tech shopping into a curated, boutique-grade digital experience.

## Colors
The color palette relies on luminous neutral fields layered under structured slate typography, accented selectively with sovereign emerald greens that communicate stability, prestige, and commercial authenticity:

- **Canvas & Surfaces:**
  - Base Canvas: `#F8FAFC` (subtle warm slate tint)
  - Surface Pure (Product Cards, Sheets, Navbars): `#FFFFFF`
  - Surface Subdued (Banners, Spec Strips, Micro-containers): `#F1F5F9`
  - Surface Selected / Highlight: `#ECFDF5`
- **Typography & Structural Slates:**
  - Ink Prominent (Headings, Prices, Key Spec Labels): `#0F172A`
  - Ink Standard (Body Copy, Secondary Navigation): `#334155`
  - Ink Subdued (Footers, Meta Details, Placeholder, Inactive Icons): `#64748B`
  - Ink Minimal (Breadcrumbs, Timestamps, Disclaimers): `#94A3B8`
- **Borders & Dividers:**
  - Subtle Border: `#F1F5F9`
  - Structural Border: `#E2E8F0`
  - Focus Ring: `#059669` with 20% opacity spread
- **Brand Accents & Feedback:**
  - Primary Emerald: `#059669` (Primary CTAs, Trust Marks, Active Filters)
  - Light Emerald: `#10B981` (Promotional Badges, In-Stock Pips, Micro-accents)
  - Dark Emerald Hover: `#047857`
  - Alert / Discount Red: `#E11D48`
  - Warning Amber: `#D97706`

## Typography
Plus Jakarta Sans delivers structural warmth and geometric sharpness for brand declarations, curated hardware editorial titles, and price points. Inter anchors reading comprehension across dense technical specifications, shipping schedules, customer reviews, and step-by-step checkout accordions.

Currency formatting follows the dedicated `price-*` tokens: the national currency signifier (SAR or ر.س) renders using `label-sm` or `label-md` seated immediately adjacent to the bold numerical figure in `Plus Jakarta Sans`, avoiding baseline drift and emphasizing value readability at a glance.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout adheres to a 12-column responsive fluid grid pinned to a maximum canvas constraint of `1440px`. 

- **Breakpoints & Layout Behavior:**
  - **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12 columns, `gutter-lg` (2rem), `margin-lg` (4rem). Product grids default to 4 columns.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8 columns, `gutter` (1.5rem), `margin` (1.5rem). Product grids collapse to 2 or 3 columns.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `gutter-sm` (1rem), `margin-sm` (1rem). Product grids display as 2 columns with reduced component padding or single-column horizontal carousels.

Spacing rhythms rely on consistent `0.5rem` (8px) multi-step increments. Section separations between featured carousels, category spotlights, and warranty reassurances command `space-xl` or larger to preserve an uncluttered atmosphere.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is realized through soft, ambient multi-layered shadows paired with hairline boundary rings (`#E2E8F0`), preventing harsh contrast lines and achieving a floating gallery feel:

- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** `#FFFFFF` surfaces bounded by an explicit `1px solid #E2E8F0` border. Applied to secondary filter wells and static table lines.
- **Level 1 (Card Resting):** `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 4px 12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03);` with a subtle `1px solid #F1F5F9` perimeter border. Standard state for product tiles, category bubbles, and customer review modules.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Active Product):** `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 2px 6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04);` with a border transition to `#CBD5E1`. Used on card hover states, floating shopping bags, and sticky buy bars.
- **Level 3 (Overlays & Dialogs):** `box-shadow: 0 16px 36px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 4px 12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06);`. Employed for quick-view panels, location delivery drawers, and checkout verification dialogs.

## Shapes
The visual identity embraces a tailored, contemporary radius structure. Base components utilize 8px (`0.5rem`), standard structural containers scale to 12px (`0.75rem`), and primary product cards and feature banners resolve at 14px–16px (`1rem`).

Micro elements—such as discount badges, stock indicators, and icon pill buttons—retain smooth pill radii (`9999px`) to contrast against the architectural geometry of product cards and photographic viewports.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Add to Cart / Proceed to Payment):** Solid emerald `#059669` fill, crisp `#FFFFFF` text (`label-lg`), height `48px`, border radius `10px`. Hover deepens to `#047857` with elevation Level 2.
- **Secondary Action (Buy Now / View Specs):** Slate `#0F172A` fill with `#FFFFFF` text. Height `48px`, border radius `10px`.
- **Tertiary / Outline:** `#FFFFFF` fill with `1px solid #E2E8F0`, `#0F172A` text. Hover adds `#F8FAFC` background and subtle `#CBD5E1` border.
- **Icon Quick Action (Wishlist, Compare):** 40px circular or 10px rounded squares, `#FFFFFF` with `1px solid #E2E8F0`, transitioning to emerald fill or outline on active engagement.

### Product Cards
- **Architecture:** Pure white container (`#FFFFFF`), `12px` border radius, `1px solid #F1F5F9`, Resting Elevation 1.
- **Image Frame:** 1:1 or 4:5 aspect ratio, clean `#F8FAFC` inner background isolating device hardware silhouettes, with top-left floating badges.
- **Badges:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padding `4px 10px`, font `label-sm`. Emerald tint (`#ECFDF5`, text `#059669`) for "Official Warranty" / "Fast Delivery"; Ruby tint (`#FFE4E6`, text `#E11D48`) for discount percentages.
- **Typography & Price Stack:** 2-line clamped product title in `body-md` (`#0F172A`), spec snippet in `body-sm` (`#64748B`), and price displayed as bold SAR value using `price-md`.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Fields:** Height `48px`, `10px` radius, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, typography `body-md` (`#0F172A`). Placeholder `#94A3B8`. Active/Focus state uses `1.5px solid #059669` with a gentle 3px emerald ring shadow.
- **Address & Payment Selectors:** Radio-bordered cards with 12px radius. When selected, border switches to `2px solid #059669` over `#F0FDF4` surface tint.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- **Filter Chips:** Height `36px`, rounded `9999px` (pill), background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, font `label-md` (`#334155`). Active/Selected chips swap to `#059669` fill with white text, or subtle `#ECFDF5` fill with `#059669` text and border.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Checkboxes:** 20px × 20px, `6px` radius, `1.5px solid #CBD5E1`. On checked: `#059669` background with white check glyph.
- **Radios:** 20px circular, `1.5px solid #CBD5E1`. On checked: white fill with a solid `#059669` center dot (8px).

### Checkout Trust Modules
- **Secure Transaction Banner:** Light `#F8FAFC` panel with `1px solid #E2E8F0`, displaying Mada, Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay, and Tabby/Tamara installment icons alongside an emerald lock icon indicating certified local transaction security.
